# Shot card

One block per shot in SHOTS.md.

```
SHOT 04
duration: 4s
start_word: when the circuit closes
in: downbeat | sentence-end | hard cut
out: match | smash | dissolve | whip
camera: slow push, 35mm, eye level
action: close-up of the Export button lighting up
identity: @ui-export (locked)
direction: viewer now knows where to click
vo: Click Export.
caption: Click Export
first_frame: path/or none
last_frame: path/or none
passport_verbatim_used: yes
verified_ui: yes | UNVERIFIED
model_hint: HyperFrames | LTX | Wan | Veo | ffmpeg-still
negative_as_positive: no extra menus, no watermark, no extra fingers
```

Fill every field. Empty fields are how drift starts.

Duration math — sum of shots = BRIEF runtime, ±10 percent. If the sum is wrong, cut shots, do not stretch stills.
