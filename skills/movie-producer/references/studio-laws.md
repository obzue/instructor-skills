# Studio laws

Four laws. Break one and consistency dies.

1. Lock assets before generation. Draft passports do not enter a shot prompt.
2. One asset, one passport. The verbatim paragraph is the memory. Do not shorten it "for the prompt."
3. Surgical edits. Change one line of a passport, bump a state id, log it. Do not silently rewrite.
4. The filesystem is the continuity department. If it is not in BRIEF, BIBLE, ASSETS, PASSPORTS, or SHOTS, it does not exist.

## Directory meaning

- BRIEF.md — job, runtime, aspect, bans
- BIBLE.md — tone, palette, type, camera defaults, reference films (links only, no ripped frames)
- ASSETS.md — inventory of stills, clips, VO, music, with rights
- PASSPORTS/ — locked descriptors
- SHOTS.md — generation order
- SELECTS/ — approved stills and clips
- GENERATIONS/ — raw model output
- FINISH/ — graded, captioned, mixed masters

## Gates

- No SHOTS.md line may name a draft passport
- No HyperFrames compose until BRIEF + SHOTS exist
- No "final" label until the review checklist in the skill body is green
