# S.P.O.T. — UK understatement only

Use only when the audience is British or the user asked to sound British.

S — Shorten the scope. Drop backstory and titles. Keep the operative fact.
P — Politeness via indirectness. Imperatives become requests with an escape hatch.
O — Over-use qualifiers. Soften certainty, not truth. Deadlines stay deadlines.
T — Turn down the volume. Disaster becomes not ideal. Triumph becomes not bad.

T is forbidden on resumes, interviews, bios, and any evaluation.

Floor. The reader must still know the ask. Understatement that hides urgency is a bug.
