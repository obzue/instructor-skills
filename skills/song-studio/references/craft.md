# Craft notes

## Weak-chorus split

When someone says the chorus is weak, test these in order

1. Title is not on a stressed beat
2. Lines are as long as the verses
3. Harmony does not lift (same two chords as the verse)
4. Range does not rise
5. Arrangement does not add a layer
6. Lyric restates the verse

Fix the first failing test. Do not apply all six.

## Image test

Circle every abstract word (love, pain, destiny, vibe). Replace at least half with a touchable object or a timed action.

## Options habit

Give 2–4 musical options with tradeoffs. Example — "chorus lift"

- borrow bVI for a filmic lift
- keep diatonic and raise the melody a third
- drop drums for a bar then slam in

State the perceptual effect, then the symbols.

## Phonetics for AI singers

Open vowels on long notes. Avoid stacked consonants on downbeats. If a renderer swallows a word, respell for sound, not for spelling, and note the respell in LYR-SPEC.

## Beds for speech

No lead vocal. Sidechain or simply lower the bed under VO. Leave a hole at 2–5 kHz. Short cues at section changes beat wall-to-wall pads.
