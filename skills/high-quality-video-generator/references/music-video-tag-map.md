# Music-video tag map

Use with the song skill global header. Do not invent a second tempo or form.

## Header (top of BRIEF.md and SHOTS.md)

```
[Style: ...]
[Tempo: ...]
[Key: ...]
[Atmosphere: ...]
```

## Section → scene

- [Intro] — cold open, no lyric captions
- [Verse n] — story coverage, word-locked captions
- [Pre-Chorus] — push-in or insert density
- [Chorus] — widest or highest-energy coverage, title allowed
- [Bridge] — contrast grade / location
- [Guitar Solo] / [Instrumental Break] — instrument hero shots, captions off
- [Drop] — hard cut on the downbeat, captions off
- [Outro] — fade, delay trails, pull-out

## Micro-timing → caption / cut

- (Spoken) — roman / smaller type, no karaoke bounce
- (Whispered) — lower opacity or tighter crop
- [Vocal Harmony: ...] — split-screen or doubled subject only if it serves the picture

## Beat math

quarter_ms = 60000 / BPM

At 74 BPM shuffle, prefer dotted-eighth cut grids only if the song tag says shuffle. At 124 BPM 4x4, cut on kick.
