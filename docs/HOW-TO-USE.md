# ObzueAI Professor — how to use

The company name is **ObzueAI**. Spell it that way in every document. Not Abzu. Not Obzue. Not Obzu.

Professor is the instructor.

## First visit

Corta greets you once. She is the interactive professor for ObzueAI. She teaches a course, reads a book, makes a documentary, and can start a framework for a podcast, a live event, or a live stream.

Choose the device and the voice, then give Corta Membrane access. Her browser opens. Add notes if you want, or tell her the project name. If she should search the web, tell her what to open. Private documents stay off until you name a file.

## What it is for

Presentations. Seminars. Colleges. Schools. Universities. Meetings. Weddings.

Professor is multi-language and stays engageable in each room. A seminar presses one claim. A school class uses plain words without talking down. A meeting names a decision. A wedding stays warm and appropriate. Accuracy does not change with the room.

## When Professor takes over

1. Open the desk. That window is the framework.
2. Load every source for the session: a public web address, a PDF link, or a text or PDF file you choose.
3. Choose the language and the room.
4. Professor teaches from what is inside the window: the reading, the graph, the review, and your claim.

Professor does not reach past that window onto a locked phone, a tablet, or a computer you did not share. If you want the real pixels of the browser tab, use **Share this tab** and accept the prompt.

## The desk

- Type an https address and press Open, or start from Spaced repetition or Bloom's taxonomy.
- Back and Forward walk pages you already opened.
- If a site refuses to be framed, the reading underneath is still the source, and its links still navigate.
- A YouTube address opens as video. A PDF address opens as a document.
- **Open a file** takes one PDF or text file you pick. It does not see the rest of the disk.
- **Film the desk** downloads a WebM of the address, the title, and the text that was actually read.
- **Share this tab** asks the browser for a pixel recording. If you decline, the desk film still works.
- **Save beats** downloads the step log.

Private networks, passwords in the address, and secrets in the beat log are refused.

## Graph, check, and review

The graph is the teaching order. A concept is not taught before what it requires. Each node carries a Bloom level: remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, create.

Due today is spaced repetition (SM-2). Show the answer, then mark Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. Ease starts at 2.5 and never falls below 1.3. A grade below 3 resets the card to one day.

## A live claim

Write one sentence you will defend. Press the claim. Professor restates it, names the Bloom level it actually reaches, says what would falsify it, gives the strongest objection, and asks one question. If a page is open, the objection has to come from that excerpt, not from an invented rest of the page.
